    Temperature Conversion Formulas & Reference

    Celsius to Others

    • Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
    • Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15

    Fahrenheit to Others

    • Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
    • Kelvin: K = ((°F − 32) × 5/9) + 273.15

    Kelvin to Others

    • Celsius: °C = K − 273.15
    • Fahrenheit: °F = ((K − 273.15) × 9/5) + 32

    Physics Boundary (Absolute Zero)

    Absolute zero is the theoretical lower limit of thermodynamic temperature scale:

    • Celsius: −273.15 °C
    • Fahrenheit: −459.67 °F
    • Kelvin: 0.00 K
